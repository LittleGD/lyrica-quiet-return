The Lyrica dial is reduced to its essential gestures: a softened indication of time, a precise circular boundary, and geometric markers that replace conventional numerals.

Nothing is added for decoration. Every mark exists for balance, rhythm, and restraint.

Observe the Details

A Lyrica watch is not understood at once. It is approached through proportion, surface, shadow, and touch.

  1. Case

    A refined steel profile with a clean architectural edge.

  2. Dial

    A frosted surface that softens the reading of time.

  3. Crown

    Compact, tactile, and engraved with restraint.

  4. Strap

    Fine-grain leather selected for softness, density, and longevity.

  5. Case Back

    Serial information, mechanical identity, and authenticity details arranged with collector-level clarity.

Mechanical by Nature
