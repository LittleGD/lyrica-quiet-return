Maison — Craft

Mechanical by nature.

A Lyrica is measured in tolerances, surfaces, and the invisible discipline behind every visible detail.

Four disciplines

Precision without performance.

Each piece passes through four hands. None of them hurry, and none of them announce themselves.

Case

Cut for quiet presence.

Brushed planes and polished edges, profile kept slim enough to sit close to the wrist.

Dial

Reduced to gesture.

A frosted surface softens the reading of time. Markers replace numerals, balance replaces decoration.

Movement

Caliber L01.

A Swiss mechanical movement, hand-regulated for a steady forty-two-hour reserve.

Strap

Soft-grain leather.

Selected for softness, density, and longevity. Stitching sits flush with the edge.

Assembly

Measured hands.

Each watch is finished and tested before it leaves the atelier in Switzerland.

Finish

Inspected in silence.

The last check is visual. The piece is held to the light, then to the wrist.

Caliber L01

A movement tuned for honest rhythm.

Self-winding mechanical caliber. Twenty-five jewels. Twenty-eight thousand eight hundred vibrations per hour. Forty-two hour power reserve. Adjusted in five positions and regulated against COSC tolerances.
